Source-native claims

  • A single welded seam proves that one local continuity claim reconciles under frozen seam accounting; it does not yet prove that continuity composes across a sequence of seams.
  • The paper constructs a bounded five-state local episode with two candidate local seams (0->2 and 2->4) and asks whether the composed transition 0->4 reconciles as one chain-level continuity claim under one frozen seam-chain contract.
  • Local welds are necessary for seam-chain continuity but not sufficient: no chain residual closure, no composed continuity.
  • Non-goal (source-stated): no new domain closure, no resistant-corpus continuity claim, no governance procedure, no universalized local return-rate closure, no portable chain law, no extension or redefinition of Tier-1, and no claim that local welds compose automatically.
  • What This Enables Next (source-stated): a small domain closure with welded transitions, a publication-infrastructure casepack using receipt chains, a repository implementation of seam-chain validation, and a failure taxonomy for chain composition; the paper enables these burdens but does not absorb them.
