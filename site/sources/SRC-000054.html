Source-native claims

  • The paper asks a complementary measurement question to non-perturbative QCD: what structural signature the quark->hadron boundary leaves when quarks and hadrons are evaluated under a single frozen particle-data closure; the kernel receives only the declared particle trace, not the QCD Lagrangian, a confining potential, Wilson loops, lattice configurations, or a running coupling.
  • Under the declared source-local closure, the mean integrity composite falls from 0.581 (quarks) to 0.0111 (hadrons), a 98.1% drop; all 14 conventional hadrons satisfy IC_h < IC_q,min = 0.517 while the arithmetic mean F drops by only 28.5%; the heterogeneity gap amplifies by 10.8x; the 14/14 bound holds across a guard-band sweep, and six exotic hadrons also fall in the hadron integrity band.
  • Source-stated non-claims: it is not an analytic proof of QCD confinement, does not solve the Yang-Mills mass-gap problem, and does not replace non-perturbative QCD; its contribution is narrower and falsifiable under the declared closure.
  • Source-stated falsifier: a new color-singlet hadron with IC >= 0.517 under the same frozen composite-particle closure would violate the universal-suppression claim of Theorem T3.
