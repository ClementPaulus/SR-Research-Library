Main question

When do individually admissible local welds compose into one admissible seam-chain rather than merely a sequence of local successes?

Scope
One bounded five-state local episode with two candidate local seams (0->2, 2->4) and their composition (0->4), evaluated under one frozen seam-chain contract for finite return, ledger change, drift and curvature debits, return credit, residual, identity, and stance.

Preserved meaning
A local weld and a seam-chain are different burdens; composed continuity requires both local seam validity and global chain reconciliation.
Distortion / substitution risk
Inferring chain continuity merely because every local transition looks acceptable or because multiple finite returns occurred.
