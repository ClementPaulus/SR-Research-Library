Main question

Which distinctions in the first atomic closure sharpen, collapse, or invert when nuclear information is added under a stronger frozen surface?

Scope
Replacement of the 8-channel surface by a 12-channel closure admitting neutron excess, binding energy per nucleon, magic-number proximity, valence-electron structure, and block order; comparison of readbacks against the first closure.
Exclusions
  • Does not replace chemistry.
  • Does not claim the periodic table originates from the kernel.
  • Does not claim the strengthened closure is simply better.
Preserved meaning
The source's mixed readback: arithmetic retention and multiplicative coherence rise while heterogeneity and curvature also rise; Collapse rows increase, Watch contracts, Stable remains absent, Critical falls.
Distortion / substitution risk
Reading the stronger closure as a superior or final atomic account rather than a more revealing and more demanding comparison surface.
