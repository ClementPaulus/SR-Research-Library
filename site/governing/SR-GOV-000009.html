Exact admitted burden

Scope. Orders Reditus in brief mathematical and Latin form as a canonically ordered treatment and local witness; declares the object, exposes its own change from the 2025 edition, and states the rules by which Reditus may be proven.
Non-goal. Neither closes the whole field nor substitutes the theory for the field; does not grant credit to the continuity between its own two editions, since that seam is not yet demonstrated.
