Exact admitted burden

Scope. The threefold authority order governing what may define, execute, validate, translate, extend, adopt, or preserve within the corpus. Functional systems govern the kind of work; authority tiers govern the kind of authority.
Non-goal. Does not specify the operating grammar of UMCP, RCFT, or ULRC (cite the Operating System papers for those); authority placement does not itself perform evaluation, discovery, or translation.

Authority scope

Tier-1 burden

  • Meaning of Tier-1: reserved kernel symbols, exact identities, and the immutable namespace; Tier-1 authority is positive as well as prohibitive and does not by itself execute a run, audit a ledger, validate a closure, or repair a translation.

Tier-0 burden

  • Meaning of Tier-0: protocol, contracts, validators, closures, ledgers, seam accounting, and stance derivation; Tier-0 does not authorize by itself.
  • Promotion limits: usefulness, fluency, or public explanation does not become canon, protocol, proof, or final stance by itself; Tier-2 does not authorize by itself.
