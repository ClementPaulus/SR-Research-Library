# Identifier Rules

- `AUTH-0001` and upward: stable author entities.
- `SR-OBJ-000001` and upward: stable research-object families.
- `SRC-000001` and upward: source objects.
- `REL-000001` and upward: typed relations.

Identifiers establish identity only. They do not encode prestige, evidence strength, truth, maturity, or authority beyond the record's explicit fields.

Revisions normally retain an ObjectID and change the version. A materially changed object, contract, authority role, or publication role requires a new ObjectID or explicit seam.
